## What it does

<AdapterClutter />

<div class="features">
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Six ports, up to 100 W each</h3>
		<p>Powers Raspberry Pis, phones, mini PCs and laptops at 5, 9, 12, 15 or 20 V, or PPS, with up to 5 A on a 5 A cable.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Shares power by priority</h3>
		<p>Set the size of your power supply and a priority for each port. When devices ask for more than the supply can give, the least important ports are turned down. Nothing is switched off.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Works with Home Assistant</h3>
		<p>Every port shows up with its power, an on/off switch and its settings, ready for dashboards, the energy panel and automations.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Status lights and live readings</h3>
		<p>A light above each port shows what it is doing, and the built-in web interface shows live power for every port.</p>
	</div>
</div>
