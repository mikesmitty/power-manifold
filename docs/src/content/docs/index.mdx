Power Manifold powers a stack of Raspberry Pis, mini PCs, phones and
laptops from one DC power supply. It replaces the separate power adapter for
each device, the power strip and the cables between them. Each of its six
USB-C ports supports USB Power Delivery, so every device gets the voltage it
requests, and a built-in controller monitors every port.

<AdapterClutter />

<div class="features">
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Six ports, up to 100 W each</h3>
		<p>Each port offers 5, 9, 12, 15 and 20 V plus PPS, and up to 5 A with a 5 A cable.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Shares power by priority</h3>
		<p>Set the size of your power supply and a priority for each port. When devices ask for more than the supply can give, the least important ports are turned down. Nothing is switched off.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Works with Home Assistant</h3>
		<p>Every port shows up with its power, an on/off switch and its settings, ready for dashboards, the energy panel and automations.</p>
	</div>
	<div class="feature">
		<h3>Status lights and live readings</h3>
		<p>A light under each port shows what it is doing, and the built-in web interface shows live power for every port.</p>
	</div>
</div>
