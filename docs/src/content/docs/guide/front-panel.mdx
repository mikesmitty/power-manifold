## Ports

Facing the front, the controller card is on the far left, with its Ethernet
port. The six USB-C ports follow it, numbered **1 to 6 from left to right**.
Each port has a status light just below and to its right.

The light belongs to the slot, not the blade, so it still tells you
something when the slot is empty.

## Port lights

| Light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="led" style="--led:#e8e8e8;opacity:.45"></span>Dim white | No port fitted in this position. |
| <span class="led blink" style="--led:#22d3ee"></span>Cyan, blinking | The blade is starting up. |
| <span class="led" style="--led:#f59e0b"></span>Amber | Ready, nothing plugged in. |
| <span class="led" style="--led:#3b82f6"></span>Blue | Powering a device at under 19 V, such as a phone or a Raspberry Pi. |
| <span class="led" style="--led:#22c55e"></span>Green | Powering a device at 19 V or more, usually a laptop. |
| <span class="led pulse" style="--led:#3b82f6"></span>Slow pulse (blue or green) | Powering a device, but turned down because the chassis is at its power budget. See [Sharing power](/power-manifold/guide/sharing-power/). |
| <span class="led" style="--led:#d946ef"></span>Magenta | The device has finished charging and is only trickling. |
| <span class="led blink" style="--led:#ef4444"></span>Red, fast blink | The port shut itself off because of a fault. It tries again after 5 seconds. See [Troubleshooting](/power-manifold/guide/troubleshooting/#a-port-blinks-red). |
| Off | The port is switched off. |

## Lights across all ports

Some patterns run across the whole row of lights:

- **A sweep from left to right** at power-up. It also confirms that every
  light is working.
- **A blue streak** crossing the row every few seconds: the controller is
  waiting for [Wi-Fi setup](/power-manifold/setup/wifi-provisioning/).
- **A magenta streak** crossing the row every few seconds: the controller
  has no API token yet and its settings are open for
  [first-time setup](/power-manifold/setup/first-time-setup/). It stops
  when you save a token.
- **A white streak** crossing the row every few seconds: the controller has
  no network connection.
- **The two halves flashing blue**: someone pressed **Identify** during
  Wi-Fi setup to find this unit.

## Brightness and dimming

You can set the light brightness in the web page's **Settings** or in Home
Assistant. At 0 the lights are off, except that a faulted port still
blinks dimly.

Two schedules can dim the lights to a low level. The default low level is
just visible in a dark room:

- **Night window**: dim between two times of day, for example 22:00 to
  07:00. This needs the clock, so set your UTC offset in **Settings**. The
  offset does not follow daylight saving time, so change it when the clocks
  change.
- **Idle dimming**: dim after a period with nothing plugged in, unplugged or
  faulted. Any change at a port brings the lights back to full.

A short press of the button brings the lights to full brightness for 30
seconds.

## The button

The button on the controller card does three things, depending on how long
you hold it:

| Press | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Short press** (under 3 seconds) | Flashes all lights white, then shows them at full brightness for 30 seconds. Useful when the lights are dimmed or off. On a controller with no API token yet, it also gives you another hour to [set one over Ethernet](/power-manifold/setup/first-time-setup/#steps). |
| **Hold 3 seconds**, then let go | Opens Bluetooth for ten minutes so you can [change Wi-Fi](/power-manifold/setup/wifi-provisioning/#changing-wi-fi-later). |
| **Hold 10 seconds** | **Factory reset.** Erases all settings and the fault log, then restarts. |

From 3 seconds on, the lights fill up in blue, one port at a time, as a
warning. If you let go while they are filling, you get the Bluetooth
window and nothing is erased. When all six are lit they turn red, and the
reset happens without waiting for you to let go.

<Aside type="caution">
A factory reset erases your Wi-Fi details, API token, port names and every
other setting. You'll need to do
[first-time setup](/power-manifold/setup/first-time-setup/) again. You can
[export your settings](/power-manifold/guide/web-page/#backing-up-your-settings)
first.
</Aside>
