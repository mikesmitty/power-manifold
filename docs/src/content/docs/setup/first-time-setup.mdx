Setup takes about ten minutes. You'll connect power, get the controller onto
your network over Ethernet or Wi-Fi, and choose a password for its settings.
You can add Home Assistant afterwards.

## What you need

- **A 24 V DC power supply** with an XT60 lead. Power Manifold is rated for
  24 V +20 %/−15 %. Choose its size by how much your devices draw in total, not by six
  times 100 W. The default power budget is 360 W (15 A at 24 V). If your
  supply is smaller, you'll lower the budget to match in step 5 below.
- **USB-C cables.** An ordinary cable carries up to 3 A (60 W). For more
  than 60 W at 20 V a device needs a 5 A cable with an e-marker chip, which
  is usually sold as a "100 W" or "240 W" cable.
- **A way onto your network.** Either an Ethernet cable from the controller
  card to your router, or a phone or computer with Bluetooth for Wi-Fi
  setup: Chrome or Edge on a desktop or Android device, or the Home
  Assistant app on an iPhone or iPad.

<Aside type="caution">
Connect the XT60 lead before you switch the supply on, and switch the
supply off before you unplug it.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Nothing can be changed until you set a token">
A new controller refuses every change that arrives over the network until
it has an API token. Its settings open only as described in step 2, and the chassis light, left
of port 1, breathes amber for as long as they are open. Saving a token
closes them and stops the light.
</Aside>

## Steps

There are two ways onto your network. Both end with saving an API token:

<SetupRoutes />

<Steps>

1. **Connect power.** Plug the power supply's XT60 lead into the socket on
   the right side of the chassis, seen from the front. The plug only fits
   one way round. Then switch the supply on. The fan blows out through the
   same side, so leave it some room.

   <SidePanel />

   The port lights sweep from left to right, then each port glows white,
   which means it is ready and waiting for a device. Ports are numbered 1
   to 6 from left to right when you face the front panel.

2. **Get it on your network.** Use whichever you have:

   - **Ethernet.** Plug a cable from the controller card's port into your
     router. The controller gets an address by itself, and for the first
     hour after power-up its settings are open to anyone on your network.
     That hour is how you get in. If it runs out, switch the supply off and
     on again, or press the button on the controller card once, and you
     have another hour.
   - **Wi-Fi.** A new controller advertises itself over Bluetooth until it
     has Wi-Fi details. Follow
     [Wi-Fi setup](/power-manifold/setup/wifi-provisioning/), either from
     that page in your browser or with the Home Assistant app. While it
     waits, the chassis light breathes blue. When
     Wi-Fi setup finishes, press **Next** on the same phone or computer. It
     opens the controller's web interface with settings unlocked for ten
     minutes.

3. **Open the web interface.** On Ethernet, go to `http://pwrman.local/`. On
   Wi-Fi, **Next** took you there already. While setup is open, the
   **Settings** panel near the bottom opens by itself. Later you can find
   the page at the same address, or at the new name if you change it.

4. **Choose an API token.** In **Settings**, set an **API token** and press
   **Save**. This is the password for changing anything from the web interface or
   the API. You'll need it on any other device, so keep it somewhere safe.
   Once the token is saved, setup is closed and the amber light stops.

5. **Set the power budget.** Still in **Settings**, set the **Chassis budget**
   to your supply's rating in watts, or a little under it. See
   [Sharing power between ports](/power-manifold/guide/sharing-power/) for
   how the budget is used.

6. **Optional: give it a name and connect Home Assistant.** Set a device
   name if you have more than one Power Manifold. Fill in your MQTT broker
   if you use Home Assistant; see
   [Home Assistant](/power-manifold/guide/home-assistant/). Press **Reboot**
   to apply a new name or broker.

</Steps>

That's it. Plug in a device and its port light turns blue or green.

## If setup closed before you saved a token

The **Settings** panel tells you which case you are in:

- **On Ethernet**, the hour is over. Switch the power supply off and on
  again, or press the button on the controller card once, then reload the
  page. You have another hour.
- **On Wi-Fi**, the ten minutes are over.
  [Open Bluetooth again](/power-manifold/setup/wifi-provisioning/#changing-wi-fi-later)
  and redo Wi-Fi setup, or plug in an Ethernet cable and restart the
  controller to use the Ethernet route instead.

## Ethernet and Wi-Fi together

When both are connected, the controller uses Ethernet and keeps Wi-Fi as a
backup. Set a fixed address if you want one. See
[Network settings](/power-manifold/integrations/network/).
